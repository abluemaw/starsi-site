# Instrument

Based on the StarSi.ai design system. The core rule: **lime means a person acts, ice blue means StarSi acts.** Buttons are lime. Links, the section labels and anything the AI does are ice blue. Surfaces are flat panels with a thin hairline, never shadows or gradients. Headlines are Mona Sans at its widest, body is Geist.

Notes for people editing this file:
- Fonts load from Google Fonts only while this design is on (`fontsUrl`).
- Dark is the default look. The light theme is for reading and print.
