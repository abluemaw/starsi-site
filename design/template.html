08 · Rules

Do and don't

Do

  • Use one gold button per view. It is the action.
  • Keep all copy exactly as written.
  • Use white text on dark and deep navy on light. Body text must pass 4.5 to 1.
  • Give every image real alt text.
  • Keep the reduced-motion fallbacks.

Don't

  • Put text over a face in a photo.
  • Use gold for body text. Gold text is for large headlines on dark only.
  • Put two gold buttons side by side.
  • Mix in the lime and ice-blue brand or the Clean White (red and navy) style.
  • Paste images into code as text. Save them in the images folder.
